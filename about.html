About

A vault, not another link dump.

OppVault is a student opportunity desk. The first door is a cached directory of public student discounts. Scholarships and fellowships get their own records next, with deadlines and official links instead of screenshots.

Student offers are live.

They come from the public StudentOffers directory, then stay searchable here. Original page and brand claim link are both shown.

Scholarships and fellowships are next.

Those backends are not built yet. The pages are here so the map is honest.

Nothing here is a guarantee.

Prices, eligibility, and deadlines move. Open the source before you apply or pay.
