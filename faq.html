FAQ

Before you claim anything

Is OppVault the brand giving the discount?

No. Student offers are cached from the public StudentOffers directory. The claim link goes to the company.

Why are scholarships empty?

That backend is next. The page is a placeholder so the nav matches the product, not a fake listing.

Are the free courses hosted here?

No. They are links to publishers that already offer a free path. OppVault does not issue those certificates.

What is the difference between Original and Claim?

Original is the StudentOffers page. Claim is the brand page where you actually redeem.

Can a deal be dead?

Yes. Always open the claim link and confirm eligibility before you tell someone it is live.
